# 2018 MCM Problem C: Traffic Flow

## Background
City planners and transportation engineers seek to understand and optimize the flow of vehicles through urban road networks. Traffic congestion causes delays, increases fuel consumption, and contributes to air pollution.

## Problem Statement

### Part A
Build a model that represents the flow of vehicles through a network of one-way streets and intersections. Your model should allow you to estimate the number of vehicles on each street and the flow rate at each intersection. Use your model to determine the capacity of a street network.

### Part B
Build a model to optimize the timing of traffic signals at a network of intersections. Your model should minimize the total delay experienced by vehicles traveling through the network. Assume that the average speed of vehicles on each street is known.

### Part C
A city is considering building a new road to reduce traffic congestion. Build a model to determine whether the new road will reduce congestion and, if so, where it should be located. Include in your model the effect of induced demand.

### Part D
Traffic flow in a city changes throughout the day. Build a model that captures the time-varying nature of traffic flow. Use your model to recommend a time-varying traffic signal schedule that optimizes flow throughout the day.

## Data
- Number of intersections: 12
- Number of streets: 18
- Average vehicle speed: 30-50 mph depending on street type
- Peak hour traffic volume: 800-1200 vehicles per hour per major street
- Signal cycle length: 90-120 seconds
