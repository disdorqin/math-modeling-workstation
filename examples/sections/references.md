# 参考文献与数据来源

本文所有数值结论均可回溯至案例目录中已登记的执行记录、数据画像与实验产物，全部产物带有内容哈希与上下游关系，可独立复现。

**数据来源**：建模数据为一份公开可获取的真实临床观测数据集，共 442 例患者，含十个基线特征与一年后的病情进展指数。该数据集最初随最小角回归方法的研究一并公开，此后被广泛用作回归方法的标准评测数据。

[1] Efron, B., Hastie, T., Johnstone, I., & Tibshirani, R. (2004). Least Angle Regression. *The Annals of Statistics*, 32(2), 407–499.

[2] Tibshirani, R. (1996). Regression Shrinkage and Selection via the Lasso. *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B*, 58(1), 267–288.

[3] Hoerl, A. E., & Kennard, R. W. (1970). Ridge Regression: Biased Estimation for Nonorthogonal Problems. *Technometrics*, 12(1), 55–67.

[4] Hastie, T., Tibshirani, R., & Friedman, J. (2009). *The Elements of Statistical Learning* (2nd ed.). Springer.

[5] Pedregosa, F., et al. (2011). Scikit-learn: Machine Learning in Python. *Journal of Machine Learning Research*, 12, 2825–2830.
